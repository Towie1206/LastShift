## 👁️ Overview

You've taken the night security guard gig at an isolated facility. Your shift runs from **12:00 AM to 6:00 AM**. 

Sounds like easy money? It's not. 

The electrical grid is on the verge of collapsing, anomalous entities are roaming through the dark hallways, and the building's ventilation system keeps shutting down. If you run out of air, you'll pass out in the dark — and whatever lurks in the facility will find you first.

---

## ⚡ Core Mechanics

### 🔋 Power & Office Defense
- **Security Doors & Hallway Lights:** Shut down security doors and flicker blindspot lights when threats approach — but watch your power consumption meter closely!
- **Blackout Protocol:** If the generator runs out of juice, all lights shut off, doors force open, and a chilling music box signals your final seconds.

### 🛠️ Maintenance & Suffocation
- **3 Critical Subsystems:** Monitor and reboot **Camera Devices**, **Facility Lighting**, and **Ventilation**.
- **Hypoxia Mechanics:** When the ventilation system fails, you have **60 seconds** to repair it. As oxygen depletes, your vision blurs, eyelids grow heavy, and the screen fades in and out of black until you lose consciousness.

### 📹 CCTV & Anomaly Hunting
- Cycle through surveillance cameras placed throughout the complex.
- Spot strange anomalies, misplaced objects, and intruders before they reach your office.

### 📱 Cross-Platform (PC & Android)
- **Windows PC:** Responsive mouse look, clickable office controls, and immersive survival gameplay.
- **Android Mobile:** Touch swipe controls to look around the office, optimized layout, and dynamic UI scaling across various smartphone aspect ratios.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | PC (Windows) | Android (Mobile) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Look Around Office** | Move Mouse Left / Right or `A` / `D` | Swipe Left / Right on screen |
| **Interact (Doors / Lights / Terminals)** | Left Click | Tap on screen |
| **Open CCTV Tablet** | Click Tablet on desk | Tap Camera button |
| **Open Maintenance PC** | Turn Left, click computer monitor | Tap Maintenance PC |
| **Exit Camera / PC View** | Click Exit button or press `S` | Tap on-screen Exit button |

## 🚀 How to Play

### 📥 Play the Builds
- **Windows PC:** Download the latest build from [Itch.io](https://itch.io/your-game-link-here), unzip the archive, and run `LastShift.exe`.
- **Android Mobile:** Download `LastShift_v0.0.2.apk` from [Itch.io](https://itch.io/your-game-link-here), install it on your device, and play!

### 💻 Open in Unity Editor
1. Clone this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Towie1206/LastShift.git
   ```
2. Open **Unity Hub** and add the project folder.
3. Recommended Unity Version: **Unity 6 (6000.5.6f1)** or newer.
4. Load the main scene: `Assets/Scenes/Game.unity`.
5. Press **Play** and survive your shift!
